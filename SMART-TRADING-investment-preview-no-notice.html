Commission rules

Level 1: $10 reward when your direct referral makes an approved deposit of at least $10.

Level 2: $3 for a qualifying deposit by a second-level referral.

Level 3: $1 for a qualifying deposit by a third-level referral.

Commissions are recorded earnings. A separate verified payout process is required before they can be withdrawn.
